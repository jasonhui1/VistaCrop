## 2024-05-15 - Accessible Icon-Only Toggles
**Learning:** Icon-only toggles (like sidebar expand/collapse buttons) in the Composer view are inaccessible to screen readers and lack visual cues for keyboard users when focused. They require explicit `aria-label`, `aria-expanded` attributes to convey state, and `aria-hidden="true"` on the purely visual SVG children, alongside clear `focus-visible` utility classes for keyboard navigation.
**Action:** When implementing new icon-only buttons or toggles in the application, always include an appropriate `aria-label`, track state using `aria-expanded` or `aria-pressed`, add `aria-hidden="true"` to child SVGs, and ensure clear visible focus rings (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`) to maintain visual and programmatic accessibility.
