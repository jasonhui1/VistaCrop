## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - Left/Right Sidebar Toggle Accessibility
**Learning:** The Left and Right sidebars in ComposerView use icon-only toggle buttons without proper accessibility attributes. Since they are structurally identical but functionally separate components, making sure both receive symmetric enhancements (aria-label, aria-expanded, and focus-visible classes for keyboard navigation, plus aria-hidden on the internal SVGs) creates a unified and accessible interface. Additionally, Playwright locators for such duplicated structural elements require `.first` and `.nth(1)` to avoid strict mode violations when fetching elements by role or aria-label.
**Action:** Always verify icon-only interactive elements for proper ARIA roles and labels. When updating layout-symmetric UI components (like Left/Right sidebars), ensure both sides are updated equally to maintain consistency. When verifying with Playwright, expect duplicate elements on the page and use specific indexing or structural locators.
